## Quick Start

1. Log in to your S1Vo account at `https://app.s1vo.com`
2. Click `+ New Doc Space` on your dashboard.
3. Enter a name for your doc space.
4. Optionally, add a description or select a color.
5. Click `Create`.

## Tips

- You can invite teammates to collaborate.
- Rename or customize your doc space at any time by clicking settings.
