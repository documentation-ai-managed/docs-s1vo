## Overview

Follow these steps to make your first doc space:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Dashboard">
    Log in on `https://app.s1vo.com` and open your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Doc Space">
    Press the `+ New Doc Space` button in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill details">
    Name your space, add a description if you like, then choose a color.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and invite">
    Hit `Create`, then invite teammates if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
